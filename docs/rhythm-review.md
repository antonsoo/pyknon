## What a learner can do

Change a six-time foot from 3+3 to 2+4 without changing its total. Reverse
up/down assignments while keeping its ratio family; join inner attacks while
keeping the defining division. Explore five, seven and eight with the scope of
Aristoxenus's three admitted genera visible. A second experiment preserves a
two-time down portion while comparing one, two and an explicitly chosen 1½
up portion. Arithmetic rationality remains distinct from the source's rhythmic
qualification.

Ten transcribed Greek passages, new English, thirty-seven contextual word
notes, a separately attributed Latin parallel and eight full 1861 pages connect
the hands-on experiment to an inspectable text. The final 'because …' is a
fragmentary break. Later Psellus and Paris witnesses printed on the same pages
are not presented as its missing continuation.

`e867a7b376ec9d41cfc2acaec81fd0fa58b538d9`, archived and built separately using
the current locked dependency directory. That old baseline build was not a
separate clean installation. The new edition has its own independent Node 24

At 375 px, Chromium and Firefox receive either a held new entry response or
an HTTP 200 containing an older entry's bytes. Pending or failed precaching
cannot establish readiness for the new page. Release or correct the response;
the complete current worker takes control and the new room becomes ready.
Chromium additionally receives an authentic old-worker reply relayed after the
new reply, within the inspector's timeout. It cannot overwrite current readiness.
An old already-open tab acknowledges the different complete edition, exposes a
reload button and then loads the five-room source. Browser-offline navigation,
new controls and the complete native reading work after the upgrade.

primary-time definition; pp.33–34 distinguish foot markers from subdivisions;
pp.34–35 retain the rhythmic qualification in the intermediate example;

inspected. The illustrated reading renders as 19 A4 PDF pages; printed pages

Greek, the Latin parallel, the preserved break and final source caption. No
JavaScript is present. Physical printing or physical listening was not performed.

The selected diagrams are temporal schematics, the clicks modern markers and
the exact midpoint a teaching choice. No recovered melody, ancient absolute
pitch, performance accent or bodily motion is claimed. The integer classifier
